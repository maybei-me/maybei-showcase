## Home vs Careers comparison evidence

The desktop comparison at 1280px confirms both routes share the same midnight navy field, blue-violet parent-brand signal, lime action treatment, lowercase maybei lockup, compact uppercase section rails and Manrope-style editorial headlines. Home is intentionally more cinematic and product-led, with the large thesis and product grid; Careers is intentionally more utilitarian and recruitment-led, with role lists, principles, benefits and the candidate form. The shared grid, typography, section numbering and footer keep them in one system while allowing distinct content density.

The 375px comparison confirms both pages stack cleanly, preserve headline hierarchy and reachable CTA targets, keep the header compact, and avoid visible horizontal overflow. Careers uses longer text blocks but maintains readable rhythm through vertical spacing and compact metadata; Home retains the product-card sequence and founders order without collapsing the brand signal.
