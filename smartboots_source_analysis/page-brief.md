# Smart Boots product page brief

## Verified product facts

Smart Boots is a football wearable concept: an embedded 9-axis IMU sensor records movement during a match, then syncs data to a mobile app after play. The supplied pitch identifies four core outcomes: activity heatmap, speed and sprint behaviour, impact / shot power, and relative team positioning. The athlete app supports player and coach contexts, while the product direction includes AI-generated, position-aware training recommendations.

## Page direction

The product page will use an original black, deep-green and lime visual system: sharp information contrast, oversized performance statements, thin instrument-like dividers and controlled acid-lime signals. The supplied Flight Fund reference informs only the high-contrast black/lime cadence and confident editorial scale; its layout, copy, information architecture and components will not be reproduced.

## Evidence asset

The supplied Smart Boots app composition is the primary product evidence. It shows the boot concept alongside English mobile screens for home performance, match overview, heatmap, performance analysis, training plan, live activity, progress and player profile.

## Page architecture

1. **Hero** — Concept-stage football performance proposition, field telemetry strip and the supplied product visual.
2. **Four signals** — Speed, field activity, strike data and team positioning, using only the verified pitch facts.
3. **Field to phone** — Player profile, sensor activation, offline match capture and post-match sync.
4. **Meaningful analysis** — Distinct player and coach outcomes with clear AI recommendation positioning.
5. **Inside the boot** — 9-axis IMU, low-power microcontroller, flash memory and wireless-charge battery.
6. **Closing statement** — A calm concept-stage call to follow the project, without inventing a live purchase, pilot or waitlist flow.

## QA record

The page was checked at 1280px and 375px. The black-and-lime system preserves readable white and muted text, while lime signals actions, status and decisive performance markers. The supplied visual makes the boot, sensor and English app product evidence visible in the hero. TypeScript, production build, 4 server unit tests and 14 Playwright UI tests pass.
